Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the scan take?

It is designed to take only a few minutes once the client is ready and the beard style is selected.

Do I need special equipment?

You do not need manufacturing equipment or inventory. The scan requires a compatible iPhone running KrezzCut.

Who handles production and shipping?

KrezzCut handles the custom production and fulfillment process.

Can every barber in the shop participate?

Yes. A shop can share one display while using individual barber codes for attribution.

Can we start with one client?

Yes. Testing the service with one engaged client is a practical way to introduce it to the shop.

What happens if a client needs support?

KrezzCut will work with the partner and client to review the order and determine the appropriate next step.
